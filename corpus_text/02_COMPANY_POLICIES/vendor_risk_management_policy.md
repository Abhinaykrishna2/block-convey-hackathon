1. Purpose

<!-- evidence:968ab61f53e92519a1e180dd source:word/document.xml#p00036 -->
This policy establishes a risk-based process for selecting, assessing, contracting with, monitoring, and offboarding vendors that may affect Solsphere AI Inc. ("Solsphere"), the Regodit service, customer data, company information, or the security and availability of company systems. It is designed to support Solsphere's commitments to customers and its SOC 2 control environment.

<!-- evidence:27bfbe5ab31f21dd34183d64 source:word/document.xml#p00037 -->
2. Scope

<!-- evidence:2ac1d7d838660f186a4ff56e source:word/document.xml#p00038 -->
This policy applies to all workforce members and to all third parties that provide products or services to Solsphere, including cloud and hosting providers, software-as-a-service providers, contractors, consultants, auditors, subprocessors, payment providers, and professional services firms.

<!-- evidence:e6d3cb86a91e5a0408223a18 source:word/document.xml#p00039 -->
The policy applies before a new vendor is engaged, throughout the relationship, when services materially change, and when the relationship ends. Commodity purchases that cannot access Solsphere information or systems and cannot materially affect operations may be documented as low risk and handled through simplified review.

<!-- evidence:3b7867e8f95ca8e2357ca460 source:word/document.xml#p00040 -->
3. Policy Principles

<!-- evidence:82189a753c6dfe05b8978cd2 source:word/document.xml#p00041 -->
Vendor access and data sharing must be limited to a documented business need.

<!-- evidence:74221f96be934f1047fc71a3 source:word/document.xml#p00042 -->
Review effort must be proportionate to the sensitivity of data, access level, operational dependency, and potential impact.

<!-- evidence:ae6fe2e2293f9bbaffd2c45c source:word/document.xml#p00043 -->
No vendor may receive production access or regulated, confidential, or customer data before required due diligence, approval, and contracting are complete, unless a documented exception is approved.

<!-- evidence:03ec7a3942628b1fc7c4c255 source:word/document.xml#p00044 -->
Solsphere remains accountable for risks arising from outsourced services and must retain sufficient oversight of critical and high-risk vendors.

<!-- evidence:f067ae574996ea38bfd00c61 source:word/document.xml#p00045 -->
Evidence of vendor reviews, approvals, contracts, monitoring, and offboarding must be retained in an auditable form.

12. Vendor Incidents

<!-- evidence:74a442ca6d2f13b9f6cd53fa source:word/document.xml#p00137 -->
Suspected vendor security, privacy, or availability incidents must be reported immediately through Solsphere's incident reporting process. Solsphere shall coordinate with the vendor to determine scope, data and systems affected, timeline, containment, evidence preservation, recovery, root cause, and corrective action. Notifications to customers, regulators, insurers, or other parties may be made only by authorized personnel in accordance with legal, contractual, privacy, and incident-response requirements.

<!-- evidence:1a173682b596625f9162341a source:word/document.xml#p00138 -->
13. Findings, Risk Acceptance, and Exceptions

<!-- evidence:a8a4cdfbd1f18e9d676cdeac source:word/document.xml#p00139 -->
Vendor findings must be risk rated and tracked to closure. An exception or risk acceptance must document the business justification, affected requirement, risk, compensating controls, owner, approver, expiration date, and review conditions. Exceptions must be time limited and reviewed before expiration. Critical unresolved risks may not be accepted without executive approval.

<!-- evidence:357f7fa3a1375eac5e35c075 source:word/document.xml#p00141 -->

<!-- evidence:099693a13464078ae6fd88ca source:word/document.xml#p00142 -->

<!-- evidence:c824c025587c5d2ef8677712 source:word/document.xml#p00143 -->

<!-- evidence:75ca65be3d88c04f013b5568 source:word/document.xml#p00144 -->

<!-- evidence:0195a019ce70c3b92e025923 source:word/document.xml#p00146 -->

<!-- evidence:cf5cd064c46d2511ed1ba16f source:word/document.xml#p00147 -->

<!-- evidence:75b3689bf28cbefebeabedf7 source:word/document.xml#p00148 -->
15. Records and Evidence Retention

<!-- evidence:aa321fbc3cdf3ae97b64afec source:word/document.xml#p00149 -->
Vendor intake records, assessments, evidence, approvals, contracts, data processing agreements, findings, monitoring records, exceptions, incident records, and offboarding evidence must be retained for the duration of the relationship and for at least three years after termination, unless a longer period is required by law, contract, litigation hold, customer commitment, or another Solsphere policy. Access to these records must be restricted to authorized personnel.

<!-- evidence:66bb350b890bedf9a7ee2f9f source:word/document.xml#p00151 -->

Suspected violations must be reported to the Information Security Lead or executive management.
