Introduction

<!-- evidence:bbb8a832fca7e44677fbf7a2 source:word/document.xml#p00022 -->
At Solsphere, our Code of Conduct is designed to provide clear and actionable guidelines for all employees, contractors, and affiliates. These standards ensure that everyone within the company acts ethically and with integrity, both professionally and personally. This document serves as a comprehensive tool to guide employees through ethical decision-making, foster a culture of respect, transparency, fairness, and accountability, and protect the company’s integrity. These policies are essential to promoting a positive workplace and ensuring compliance with laws and company guidelines.

<!-- evidence:0eae94a11065702d7f94d9c9 source:word/document.xml#p00023 -->
The Code of Conduct applies to all personnel at all levels of the organization. Whether directly employed or contracted, all individuals working on behalf of Solsphere must adhere to these principles in every aspect of their business interactions.

<!-- evidence:59ad9a8549c201818aea97d9 source:word/document.xml#p00025 -->
1. Ethical Standards for Employee Behavior

<!-- evidence:eeda66765c09faf302fcdca5 source:word/document.xml#p00026 -->
Honesty, Integrity, and Respect
Employees must uphold honesty in every communication, action, and decision, ensuring that they are truthful and transparent with colleagues, clients, partners, and customers. Integrity is central to our culture; it means doing the right thing even in the absence of supervision. Employees should act ethically, keep their commitments, and take responsibility for their actions. Respect involves treating others with dignity, valuing diversity, and refraining from behaviors that could be perceived as offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory. All employees should engage in decision-making processes that reflect the highest ethical standards.

<!-- evidence:8c341dd3bafd081fcaf2ab69 source:word/document.xml#p00027 -->
If an employee is uncertain about the ethical implications of an action or decision, they should consult with management or the ethics committee for guidance.

<!-- evidence:b61866c8be05744650eac2bc source:word/document.xml#p00028 -->
Professional Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism in all areas, including verbal and non-verbal communication. This includes respecting differences of opinion and maintaining a courteous tone during conversations, even in stressful situations. Unprofessional behavior, such as offensive language, public disagreements, or inappropriate conduct, will not be tolerated.

<!-- evidence:c52223638e28bf6ad7087f57 source:word/document.xml#p00030 -->
2. Non-Discrimination, Harassment, and Bullying

<!-- evidence:861452846356eb897c3cb5f4 source:word/document.xml#p00031 -->
Policy on Discrimination, Harassment, and Bullying
Solsphere is committed to providing a workplace that is free from all forms of discrimination, harassment, and bullying. Employees must treat one another with dignity, respect, and fairness, regardless of age, gender, race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or nationality. Discrimination in any form, whether subtle or overt, is strictly prohibited. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment and can take physical, verbal, or visual forms. Employees must not engage in bullying behaviors that undermine or harm colleagues’ emotional, physical, or social well-being.

<!-- evidence:05c0e517972b99a032fea4b0 source:word/document.xml#p00032 -->
Equal Opportunity and Fair Treatment
 At Solsphere, we are committed to ensuring all employment decisions—hiring, promotions, training, and compensation—are based solely on qualifications and performance. All employees are entitled to equal treatment and opportunity, free from bias, prejudice, or favoritism. Our hiring and promotion processes are designed to recognize individual merit and capabilities.

3. Conflicts of Interest

<!-- evidence:67a2a6cd36048f9f660afc9a source:word/document.xml#p00035 -->
Disclosure and Management of Conflicts of Interest
A conflict of interest occurs when an employee’s personal interests interfere with their ability to act in the best interest of Solsphere. Conflicts may be financial, personal, or relational in nature. It is crucial that employees disclose potential conflicts, whether real or perceived, to the Senior Management and / or Head of Human Resources. This transparency ensures that conflicts are addressed in a timely and effective manner. For example, if an employee holds a financial interest in a supplier or vendor, they must disclose this information and recuse themselves from related decision-making processes.

<!-- evidence:e8cff3ea21f50a24a2ddc8bf source:word/document.xml#p00036 -->
Avoiding Conflicts of Interest
Employees should always prioritize the interests of Solsphere over personal gain. Avoiding situations where personal relationships or financial interests could compromise or even appear to compromise professional decision-making is critical. Employees must also refrain from engaging in personal business transactions that could create a conflict or the perception of bias in their professional role.

<!-- evidence:8cbbd54824173a2c92f53948 source:word/document.xml#p00038 -->
4. Gifts, Hospitality, and Benefits

<!-- evidence:52aa822d0b12e8c9bab83b45 source:word/document.xml#p00039 -->
Guidelines on Accepting and Giving Gifts
Employees must avoid accepting or offering gifts, hospitality, or other benefits that could influence, or be perceived to influence, business decisions. Modest gifts or hospitality (e.g., a meal or small token of appreciation) may be acceptable, but lavish or frequent exchanges are discouraged. Gifts intended to establish a personal relationship or influence decisions could raise ethical concerns. Employees should use their discretion when evaluating such situations.

<!-- evidence:08093c7c9b4d2db74cefc5a3 source:word/document.xml#p00040 -->
Disclosure Requirements
Employees must report any gifts or benefits they receive or offer that exceed a specific threshold. Reports should be made to the compliance department or human resources. Any gift exceeding the specified value will be reviewed for potential conflicts of interest or ethical concerns. Transparency ensures that the company can maintain its commitment to fairness and impartiality.

<!-- evidence:3c0f0e049867084b3a6ebbb4 source:word/document.xml#p00041 -->
Business Meals and Entertainment
Business meals and entertainment are acceptable only when they are reasonable, appropriate for the context, and not intended to influence decision-making. When engaging in these activities, employees should always act in accordance with the company’s values and adhere to reasonable limits.

<!-- evidence:aedbe1181e058915b6a48c34 source:word/document.xml#p00043 -->
5. Insider Trading and Confidential Information

<!-- evidence:baa92bfc1ce16e4418b970be source:word/document.xml#p00044 -->
Prohibition of Insider Trading
Insider trading is the illegal practice of using non-public, material information to make financial gains in securities trading. Employees are prohibited from using or disclosing confidential company information for personal profit or to assist others in doing so. Violations of insider trading laws can lead to severe legal and financial consequences, as well as termination of employment.

<!-- evidence:fe19455bdd2b2335025e6cca source:word/document.xml#p00045 -->
Protection of Confidential Information
Confidentiality is essential in maintaining Solsphere’s competitive advantage and ensuring the trust of clients and partners. Employees must protect proprietary information, intellectual property, and trade secrets. Employees are expected to keep confidential information within the company, ensuring that only authorized individuals with a legitimate need to know have access to sensitive data.

<!-- evidence:bf4681d602c3c93264731f40 source:word/document.xml#p00046 -->
Social Media and External Communications
Employees must refrain from discussing sensitive or confidential information on social media platforms. Any communication with the media or external parties representing the company must be authorized by the communications team to ensure consistency and protect the company’s interests.

<!-- evidence:42032d5787f24d778cc278ba source:word/document.xml#p00048 -->
6. Protection of Sensitive Information

<!-- evidence:439b6b7c971c56d40651cb15 source:word/document.xml#p00049 -->
Responsibility for Information Security
Employees must comply with the company's cybersecurity protocols and ensure that all sensitive data, including personal, financial, and proprietary information, is protected. This includes using strong passwords, securing devices, and reporting any potential breaches immediately. Cybersecurity is a shared responsibility, and all employees must be vigilant to safeguard company data.

<!-- evidence:9afb445fc5adcc9d9b5567b5 source:word/document.xml#p00050 -->
Sensitive Customer and Employee Data
Solsphere is committed to adhering to applicable data protection regulations such as the GDPR or CCPA. Employees must handle all personal data with care, ensuring that it is stored, shared, and disposed of in accordance with legal requirements. Employees are prohibited from accessing personal data unless they require it to perform their job duties.

7. Prohibition of Bribery, Corruption, and Facilitation Payments

<!-- evidence:d338634b4a60c87551ae38a6 source:word/document.xml#p00053 -->
Zero-Tolerance for Corruption
Bribery, corruption, and facilitation payments undermine trust and fairness in business operations. Employees are prohibited from offering or accepting bribes, kickbacks, or any form of illegal inducement, whether monetary or otherwise. Employees must not engage in any behavior that could be perceived as corrupt, including making payments to government officials or vendors to expedite services.

<!-- evidence:144eae63c367e108cd0405d7 source:word/document.xml#p00054 -->
Reporting Corruption
Any employee who suspects bribery, corruption, or facilitation payments should report these concerns immediately. The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for corruption and is committed to investigating all claims thoroughly. Employees who participate in such activities will face disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
