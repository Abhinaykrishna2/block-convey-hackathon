1. Purpose

<!-- evidence:4cedd69829d58f29110dd940 source:word/document.xml#p00020 -->
This policy establishes the framework by which Solsphere identifies, classifies, responds to, communicates about, and learns from information security and availability incidents. It is designed to ensure a consistent, timely, and effective response that limits impact to Solsphere, its clients, and its data, and to satisfy the incident management requirements of SOC 2 (Security and Availability Trust Services Criteria) 

<!-- evidence:8f04958b5674037f831c11c5 source:word/document.xml#p00021 -->
2. Scope

<!-- evidence:dbc62fa49176af3864c91f65 source:word/document.xml#p00022 -->
This policy applies to all incidents that affect the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Solsphere systems, data, or the systems/data of clients processed on Solsphere's behalf. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to Solsphere or client environments.

<!-- evidence:d90841862d8b4ec77bb774f4 source:word/document.xml#p00023 -->
3. Definitions

<!-- evidence:0aaec10eee2b78a9c847fbb4 source:word/document.xml#p00024 -->
Event: Any observable occurrence in a system or network (e.g., a login, a failed process, a firewall alert).

<!-- evidence:3a3d24d020a3b3d1550d3e72 source:word/document.xml#p00025 -->
Incident: An event, or series of events, that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or systems, or that violates policy.

<!-- evidence:5fd27fd0ec07d82399d33217 source:word/document.xml#p00026 -->
Breach: An incident confirmed to have resulted in unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of confidential, personal, or regulated data.

<!-- evidence:2d6ff435f293602b3db56448 source:word/document.xml#p00027 -->
Incident Response Team (IRT): The designated group of individuals responsible for managing the response to an incident.

<!-- evidence:84d569c785557f07bff8eb89 source:word/document.xml#p00028 -->
4. Roles and Responsibilities

<!-- evidence:d0338e3a526f4d640ee37d12 source:word/document.xml#p00029 -->
IRT Lead / Information Security Officer: Owns the incident end-to-end, coordinates the response, and has authority to declare an incident and its severity. Also manages internal updates and drafts external/client/regulator communications for approval.

<!-- evidence:b5da2ca2b7b4aca1f43ab699 source:word/document.xml#p00031 -->
Senior Management: Informed for SEV-1/SEV-2 incidents; approves external communications and any regulatory filings.

6. Detection and Reporting

<!-- evidence:20980b0f3146a93883281efd source:word/document.xml#p00055 -->
Incidents may be identified through automated monitoring and alerting, employee or contractor reports, client reports, or third-party notifications (e.g., vendor breach disclosures).

<!-- evidence:a584be0e5da9e9dc301162cf source:word/document.xml#p00056 -->
All employees and contractors must report a suspected incident to the IRT lead as soon as possible and no later than 1 hour after discovery.

<!-- evidence:e7df479282942552ef69cc7a source:word/document.xml#p00057 -->
The IRT Lead validates the report, opens a formal incident record, and assigns an initial severity classification within 30 minutes of report.

<!-- evidence:39575f19f5c87d3f77b7626c source:word/document.xml#p00058 -->
An incident log (timeline, actions taken, decisions, evidence references) is maintained for every SEV-0/SEV-1 incident.

<!-- evidence:2a12365426a671b67ad4261b source:word/document.xml#p00059 -->
7. Incident Response Lifecycle

<!-- evidence:047915a33c9048f55241ae77 source:word/document.xml#p00060 -->
Once declared, an incident proceeds through the following phases. Phases may overlap or iterate depending on incident complexity.

<!-- evidence:03c7da5ad84ca1214de0f992 source:word/document.xml#p00061 | table 3, XML row 1, XML cell 1 -->

1. Preparation

<!-- evidence:21aa47daba8a9de714dcef9e source:word/document.xml#p00064 | table 3, XML row 2, XML cell 2 -->
Maintain IRT roster, contact lists, tooling, playbooks, and this policy; conduct periodic training.

<!-- evidence:4a1f30e0678ebf775e906dad source:word/document.xml#p00065 | table 3, XML row 3, XML cell 1 -->
2. Detection & Analysis

<!-- evidence:e180ec1b4c86ad394a8bb629 source:word/document.xml#p00066 | table 3, XML row 3, XML cell 2 -->
Identify and validate the incident via monitoring alerts, employee reports, or third-party/client reports; assign severity; open an incident ticket/record.

<!-- evidence:d0959b3ca3b0ec33a4a2bf4a source:word/document.xml#p00067 | table 3, XML row 4, XML cell 1 -->
3. Containment

<!-- evidence:7070679974f18c90f857c1b4 source:word/document.xml#p00068 | table 3, XML row 4, XML cell 2 -->
Short-term containment to limit damage (e.g., isolate host, revoke credentials, disable account); long-term containment while remediation is prepared.

<!-- evidence:2bb85058d1aa2ebd8d1eeec3 source:word/document.xml#p00069 | table 3, XML row 5, XML cell 1 -->
4. Eradication

<!-- evidence:3373cc69dc0894723efc1ed9 source:word/document.xml#p00070 | table 3, XML row 5, XML cell 2 -->
Remove the root cause (malware, vulnerability, unauthorized access) from the environment.

<!-- evidence:ecc6d2df883abe35e2c000ea source:word/document.xml#p00071 | table 3, XML row 6, XML cell 1 -->
5. Recovery

<!-- evidence:d8d540eee56e3077a16cdb3e source:word/document.xml#p00072 | table 3, XML row 6, XML cell 2 -->
Restore affected systems to normal operation; monitor closely for recurrence before closing the incident.

<!-- evidence:6282812d3ac1ff5c875ba2a5 source:word/document.xml#p00073 | table 3, XML row 7, XML cell 1 -->
6. Post-Incident Review

<!-- evidence:435607c47625f16d49b64218 source:word/document.xml#p00074 | table 3, XML row 7, XML cell 2 -->
Conduct a retrospective, document root cause and timeline, assign corrective actions, and update the risk register.

<!-- evidence:f267f26be83bd0e83b9fb04a source:word/document.xml#p00075 -->
8. Communication Plan

<!-- evidence:8953287c37c12352807e5171 source:word/document.xml#p00076 -->
Internal communication: The IRT Lead provides status updates to the Senior Management and relevant stakeholders at a cadence appropriate to severity

<!-- evidence:028836cfa47b620071b7b84d source:word/document.xml#p00077 -->
Client communication: Where an incident affects a client's data or service, Solsphere will notify the client in accordance with the notification timeline and method specified in the applicable client contract/DPA.

<!-- evidence:876c926d6c285d6107c2bf21 source:word/document.xml#p00078 -->
Regulatory notification: Where an incident constitutes a reportable breach of personal data, Solsphere will assess and comply with applicable notification obligations.

<!-- evidence:7de42df61108c1c07afedb8e source:word/document.xml#p00079 -->
Spokesperson: All external and media communications are approved by and issued through Senior Management (CEO, CBO) only

<!-- evidence:89160cf51e0fbf8394ea7779 source:word/document.xml#p00080 -->
9. Evidence Handling and Chain of Custody

<!-- evidence:aa0d9db0f3751c6fbd9ee1f1 source:word/document.xml#p00081 -->
Relevant logs, system images, and artifacts must be preserved in their original state as soon as an incident is declared, before any remediation that could alter or destroy evidence.

<!-- evidence:f1f16dabdfdbd1632570e229 source:word/document.xml#p00082 -->
Access to preserved evidence is restricted to the IRT and logged.

<!-- evidence:0ebc9dbc4900f6ce7a2f256c source:word/document.xml#p00083 -->
Where law enforcement or legal action is reasonably anticipated, evidence handling follows chain-of-custody practices sufficient to preserve admissibility.

<!-- evidence:07c505ae3bf6487e13e6c7da source:word/document.xml#p00084 -->
10. Post-Incident Review

<!-- evidence:20dc2d768d66b400305e8490 source:word/document.xml#p00085 -->
Within 5 business days of resolution of any SEV-0 or SEV-1 incident, the IRT conducts a post-incident review to document the root cause, full timeline, effectiveness of the response, and corrective actions. Corrective actions are tracked to closure and, where they indicate a gap in controls, fed back into the risk register and risk treatment plan.

<!-- evidence:3f0a23aa6a56ccd27edf3e12 source:word/document.xml#p00086 -->
11. Testing and Training

<!-- evidence:73a37dc245b3fd0e42ee41aa source:word/document.xml#p00087 -->
All employees receive security awareness training, including how to recognize and report a suspected incident at onboarding and annually thereafter

<!-- evidence:1f8232a20d2536e9ed8094b8 source:word/document.xml#p00088 -->
Lessons learned from tabletop exercises and real incidents are used to update this policy and associated playbooks.

<!-- evidence:26a0ffa977207e210153ef80 source:word/document.xml#p00089 -->

<!-- evidence:e831430d4c519fd28fe56a77 source:word/document.xml#p00090 -->
