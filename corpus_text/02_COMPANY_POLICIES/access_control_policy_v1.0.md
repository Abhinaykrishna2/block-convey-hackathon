1. Purpose and Scope

<!-- evidence:cb3dd1837395c23a89d6030c source:word/document.xml#p00019 -->
This Access Control Policy establishes how access to the company’s information systems, applications, and data is granted, managed, and revoked, following the principles of least privilege and need-to-know. It applies to all founders, employees, interns, contractors, and third parties, and to all logical access to the company’s systems, cloud platforms, and applications.

<!-- evidence:844b81804abf2353ca39023f source:word/document.xml#p00020 -->
The policy is proportionate to a small, cloud-native, remote-first team and works alongside the Information Security Policy, the Password/Secrets Policy, the Human Resource Policy, the Data Classification Policy, and the Incident Management Policy.

<!-- evidence:7778544cbd818df84e62c097 source:word/document.xml#p00021 -->
2. Roles and Responsibilities

<!-- evidence:c599a926147e7363cdf796e8 source:word/document.xml#p00022 -->
The Chief Executive Officer, acting as Chief Information Security Officer, owns this policy and the access model, and leads the annual access review.

<!-- evidence:9d695e7f8870feed6138e0e8 source:word/document.xml#p00023 -->
The Chief Technology Officer administers access provisioning, changes, and revocation across systems, holds standing production access, and approves production-access grants.

<!-- evidence:d1383a0f5147795cd03ee840 source:word/document.xml#p00024 -->
The Chief Business Officer / Chief Privacy Officer owns non-technical access matters and approves access to business and people systems.

<!-- evidence:8f083c2a773aba1ce9c35d0b source:word/document.xml#p00025 -->
All users use only authorized credentials, protect them, and report any suspicious activity or unauthorized access.

<!-- evidence:ce8820038db2348871ef5586 source:word/document.xml#p00026 -->
3. User Access Provisioning

<!-- evidence:7ed9a3138b553375ec19fef1 source:word/document.xml#p00027 -->
New access requests are approved by the relevant lead and granted on a least-privilege basis according to role. Provisioning is executed by the designated administrator. Access granted at onboarding is aligned with the onboarding process defined in the Human Resource Policy.

<!-- evidence:e05db08eb13df66474e7595b source:word/document.xml#p00028 -->
4. Authentication

<!-- evidence:72b37f5e755efac2aa8cd84d source:word/document.xml#p00029 -->
Multi-factor authentication is enforced across all core systems, including cloud infrastructure consoles, the identity and email provider, and the source-code platform. Password construction, rotation, and storage requirements are defined in the Password/Secrets Policy.

<!-- evidence:f7d9a1a10cd8fc37801196e3 source:word/document.xml#p00030 -->
5. Privileged and Production Access

<!-- evidence:18b79a216c95d721d5fd50f1 source:word/document.xml#p00031 -->
Standing production access is limited to the Chief Technology Officer. Other personnel obtain production access from the Chief Technology Officer on a need basis, granted for the duration required. Any production change requires approval from the Chief Technology Officer, the Chief Business Officer / Chief Privacy Officer, or the Chief Executive Officer. Privileged actions are logged.

<!-- evidence:caf856d1611c3505e7c0f2e0 source:word/document.xml#p00032 -->
6. Inactive Accounts

<!-- evidence:760848b02fe2707dd7909ff9 source:word/document.xml#p00033 -->
Accounts that have been inactive for 90 days are disabled. This is currently performed manually, with automation planned.

<!-- evidence:85bf2322cc403e2ed333391b source:word/document.xml#p00034 -->
7. Access Reviews

<!-- evidence:260b2de8b571f212dc9da561 source:word/document.xml#p00035 -->
Access to all systems is reviewed at least annually across all users to confirm that privileges remain appropriate to each person’s role. Findings are documented and remediated, and unnecessary access is removed.

<!-- evidence:7afdf43138b14a4161524765 source:word/document.xml#p00036 -->
8. Separation of Duties

<!-- evidence:8c89d6a1fe7665e37db343e6 source:word/document.xml#p00037 -->
Segregation of duties is applied where feasible; the company acknowledges that team size limits full separation. Rather than enforcing a strict separation between development and production, the company restricts standing production access to the Chief Technology Officer, logs production activity, and gates production changes through the approval requirement in Section 5.

<!-- evidence:2d86463d0c55fc444e7d41c9 source:word/document.xml#p00038 -->
9. Remote Access

<!-- evidence:85990614203d3b31d51fc847 source:word/document.xml#p00039 -->
Remote access to internal resources is being consolidated onto the company VPN, protected by multi-factor authentication. Endpoints used for remote access must meet the company’s security requirements, and sessions time out after a period of inactivity.

<!-- evidence:fcbb79e3eb5f6c3c789a915c source:word/document.xml#p00040 -->
10. Third-Party and Vendor Access

<!-- evidence:c20c2e59db466a519f13e3f9 source:word/document.xml#p00041 -->
Vendors and contractors receive access only on the basis of business necessity and least privilege. Contracts include access-control and security obligations, and vendor access is included in the annual access review. Vendor risk is further governed by the Risk Management Policy.

<!-- evidence:905409d4f32138cc17596968 source:word/document.xml#p00042 -->
11. Access Revocation and Offboarding

<!-- evidence:22dc80fe35a961d46a31d002 source:word/document.xml#p00043 -->
On termination or offboarding, access is revoked promptly. Revocation is requested through the company’s internal channel and executed by the designated administrator, with the Chief Technology Officer accountable for completion, aligned with the offboarding checklist in the Human Resource Policy. Temporary access permissions expire at their defined end.

<!-- evidence:5a4c411dce859070bf393313 source:word/document.xml#p00044 -->
12. Logging and Monitoring of Access

<!-- evidence:a354dfd3ad9caa681d25cced source:word/document.xml#p00045 -->
Access and privileged activity are logged using cloud-native logging. The cloud provider’s logging service retains logs for approximately one year, and logs shipped to object storage are retained on a long-term basis. Logs are reviewed for anomalies and unauthorized access attempts.

<!-- evidence:26b8edad04098788f533f192 source:word/document.xml#p00046 -->
13. Integration with Other Policies

<!-- evidence:893ebea4392cdd6ebff072d0 source:word/document.xml#p00047 -->
This policy implements, in operational detail, the identity and authorization requirements stated in the Information Security Policy. It works most closely with the Password and Secrets Policy, which governs the credentials, secrets, and authentication factors that this policy relies on to establish identity; the two are intended to be read together.

<!-- evidence:0b5d790235759e0cc0e7ed10 source:word/document.xml#p00048 -->
What a person may reach is determined by the sensitivity of the data involved, as defined by the Data Classification Policy, and applied to the systems and devices recorded under the Asset Management Policy. Access to cryptographic keys and secrets is granted under this policy and protected as set out in the Cryptography Policy. Provisioning at onboarding and revocation at departure are steps within the processes defined in the Human Resource Policy, and the conduct expected of an account holder is set out in the Code of Conduct Policy.

<!-- evidence:2a59199c98ba371e04ac0aed source:word/document.xml#p00049 -->
Unauthorized access, whether suspected or confirmed, is handled as an event under the Incident Management Policy, and access-related exposures that cannot be immediately resolved are recorded and treated under the Risk Management Policy. The approval requirement for production changes stated here is also relied on by the Vulnerability and Patch Management Policy and by the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy.

<!-- evidence:2cd8c0ddc2eea036d0a4dddf source:word/document.xml#p00050 -->
14. Compliance and Review

<!-- evidence:a1ed1ebb9257a0d6859b243c source:word/document.xml#p00051 -->
Access-control practices support the company’s SOC 2 objectives. This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated as the company grows or its controls change.

<!-- evidence:146c49758024327f04008320 source:word/document.xml#p00052 -->
15. Policy Acknowledgment

<!-- evidence:7cc8c95bf06749ca57433b3e source:word/document.xml#p00053 -->
All personnel are required to acknowledge this policy at onboarding and on significant updates. Violations may result in disciplinary action under the Human Resource Policy, up to and including termination.
