1. Purpose and Scope

<!-- evidence:bf0267f0a7bfa86ff823cb82 source:word/document.xml#p00021 -->
The purpose of this Human Resources Policy is to define the expectations, responsibilities, and procedures related to the secure and compliant management of personnel throughout their employment lifecycle. This policy establishes the requirements necessary to mitigate risks posed by human factors and to ensure alignment with organizational goals, industry regulations, and legal obligations. It addresses both internal staff and third-party personnel who have system access, regardless of their location or mode of engagement.

<!-- evidence:c305502839a994fe1a548397 source:word/document.xml#p00022 -->
This policy is applicable to all full-time and part-time employees, contractors, consultants, interns, and temporary workers. It governs key activities such as recruitment, onboarding, role definition, performance evaluations, policy adherence, security training, and access management. The scope extends across all departments and operational units within the company, including third parties with privileged access.

<!-- evidence:2afc74d331bd823167cbc710 source:word/document.xml#p00024 -->
2. Roles and Organizational Responsibilities

<!-- evidence:a9acb83af74dea99477734a6 source:word/document.xml#p00025 -->
The organization maintains a structured and transparent framework outlining specific roles and responsibilities for personnel involved in HR and compliance. The People Operations Officer plays a central role in developing, implementing, and managing HR policies. This individual is tasked with overseeing day-to-day HR functions, maintaining employee records, coordinating onboarding and offboarding processes, and facilitating internal communications regarding policy and role updates.

<!-- evidence:9ee1356bea75fd65ee46155e source:word/document.xml#p00026 -->
The Compliance Manager is responsible for aligning HR procedures with applicable legal and regulatory standards. This includes conducting periodic audits, reviewing training compliance, ensuring that policies reflect legal updates, and advising on matters related to contractual obligations. Both roles collaborate to promote a secure and legally compliant working environment.

<!-- evidence:0ee02a00c02efb33775a3a48 source:word/document.xml#p00027 -->
To maintain clarity and reduce ambiguity, the organization ensures that an official and updated organizational structure is maintained. This structure clearly defines reporting lines, departmental responsibilities, and individual role descriptions. The organizational chart is reviewed biannually, with mandatory updates following significant staffing or structural changes.

<!-- evidence:a3f599d60ff9bb76a984246d source:word/document.xml#p00029 -->
3. Recruitment and Hiring Procedures

<!-- evidence:f5e4eb9ef20f25450030ba65 source:word/document.xml#p00030 -->
The hiring process is meticulously designed to ensure that individuals selected for roles are qualified, trustworthy, and aligned with the organization’s standards. All job postings and requisitions must include detailed descriptions of role responsibilities, essential skills, required certifications, and expectations regarding conduct and data handling.

<!-- evidence:c4e2c5e3fa79ef72c01982c7 source:word/document.xml#p00031 -->
As a foundational control, all prospective employees and third-party personnel with system or data access must undergo comprehensive background checks. These checks include, but are not limited to, verification of identity, education, past employment, criminal records (as permitted by law), and professional certifications. The depth of the background verification is scaled in proportion to the sensitivity of the role in question.

<!-- evidence:32b3283e2a4be82575c3b30f source:word/document.xml#p00032 -->
Candidate assessments are carried out through a standardized process which may involve structured interviews, technical evaluations, reference checks, and practical assessments tailored to the position. This standardization ensures fairness, accountability, and effective risk mitigation during talent acquisition.

<!-- evidence:14cb9d5f88f6e6aba9e5fae4 source:word/document.xml#p00035 -->
4. Onboarding and Communication of Responsibilities

<!-- evidence:09d7eb531903380fbb0a7948 source:word/document.xml#p00036 -->
Upon selection, all new hires and contractors undergo an onboarding program that familiarizes them with the organization’s policies, security expectations, and their specific responsibilities. Each individual is formally provided with their job description and a copy of relevant policies, including the Code of Conduct, Acceptable Use Policy, and this HR Policy.

<!-- evidence:b29317f0b945e43a1004504d source:word/document.xml#p00037 -->
Additionally, all personnel are required to acknowledge in writing their understanding of and agreement to adhere to company rules, confidentiality standards, and data protection responsibilities. This acknowledgment is a condition of continued employment or contract execution.

<!-- evidence:551ea8681bf0f7ac78f23b10 source:word/document.xml#p00038 -->
Where access to confidential or sensitive data is granted, the individual must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). The NDA defines the scope of confidentiality, outlines handling procedures for sensitive data, and details post-employment obligations. No access to internal systems or customer information is provided until these agreements are completed. The full onboarding checklist, covering pre-onboarding, access provisioning, and Day-1 steps, is provided in Annexure A: Employee Onboarding Checklist.

5. Performance Evaluation and Security Compliance

<!-- evidence:789d2e3a70dec1557ba97661 source:word/document.xml#p00041 -->
All employees undergo annual performance evaluations that assess not only their achievement of job-related objectives but also their adherence to organizational policies, ethical behavior, and information security responsibilities. These reviews provide structured feedback and identify areas for improvement or additional training.

<!-- evidence:95d2b9d0d3f16cf8d0eba723 source:word/document.xml#p00042 -->
The evaluation process is conducted in alignment with organizational goals and includes input from direct supervisors, HR, and if applicable, the Compliance department. It ensures that employees remain aligned with role expectations, receive recognition for high performance, and are accountable for any deviations or compliance failures.

<!-- evidence:23633d8bd8e93353ecd12213 source:word/document.xml#p00043 -->
Security responsibilities are a core component of the evaluation framework, and persistent failure to meet these standards may result in corrective action, retraining, or disciplinary procedures.

<!-- evidence:966bdb0a7deaae5fba82a38b source:word/document.xml#p00046 -->
6. Training and Awareness

<!-- evidence:b6750c483ceba6bde7b8135e source:word/document.xml#p00047 -->
All personnel, including contractors and third-party users with access to critical systems or data, must complete a formal information security awareness training program upon joining the organization. This training covers key areas such as password hygiene, phishing awareness, secure data handling, and regulatory compliance.

<!-- evidence:649c1bb90d7df07b2c39f956 source:word/document.xml#p00048 -->
The training is repeated annually to reinforce knowledge and to account for evolving security threats. Attendance and completion records are monitored by the HR department, and failure to comply with training requirements within a defined time frame may lead to access suspension or disciplinary action.

<!-- evidence:cf131b35a782a3ae88e9f899 source:word/document.xml#p00049 -->
To ensure the effectiveness of training programs, periodic reviews are conducted to update the curriculum based on recent incidents, changes in policy, or regulatory guidance.

<!-- evidence:030b3f8583c31a4bce8f8770 source:word/document.xml#p00052 -->
7. Policy Communication and Exception Handling

<!-- evidence:00b721978edd0844acad94dd source:word/document.xml#p00053 -->
The company maintains clear communication channels to ensure that all staff are aware of their responsibilities and organizational policies. Policies are disseminated through internal HR portals, email communications, and in-person briefings where necessary. Employees are encouraged to seek clarification on any aspect of a policy that is unclear.

<!-- evidence:0711edd4ce664b540b827dd6 source:word/document.xml#p00054 -->
In cases where exceptions to this policy are required, a formal exception request process is in place. Requests must be submitted in writing to the Lead HR Manager and must include justification, associated risks, and proposed compensating controls. Each request is reviewed by both HR and Compliance, and decisions are documented for accountability.

<!-- evidence:e98d910f91c34618768c4116 source:word/document.xml#p00055 -->
Approved exceptions must be formally recorded and retained for audit purposes. Each exception shall be assigned a validity period and must be reassessed upon expiration or as part of the annual policy review. Unauthorized exceptions are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

<!-- evidence:d84d3a2e331c3e28d33b5989 source:word/document.xml#p00056 -->
Employees must be notified promptly of any updates to this policy. Significant revisions trigger a mandatory acknowledgment process where employees confirm they have read and understood the changes.

<!-- evidence:bace894046a7570ab846fe95 source:word/document.xml#p00058 -->
8. Disciplinary Action and Enforcement

<!-- evidence:24a810d65fd753adc5237bfb source:word/document.xml#p00059 -->
Violations of this policy or related policies are treated with seriousness. A formal disciplinary framework exists to handle such violations, ranging from verbal or written warnings to suspension and termination, depending on the nature and severity of the incident.

<!-- evidence:bdc557a4cacd1d34df569f23 source:word/document.xml#p00060 -->
Immediate suspension of access privileges is mandated in cases involving sensitive policy breaches, and investigations are conducted in coordination with HR, Compliance, and IT Security. The findings inform disciplinary actions and are documented to maintain fairness and legal defensibility.

<!-- evidence:ebc6f4d01686834cda33f331 source:word/document.xml#p00061 -->
To encourage responsible conduct and reporting, the company fosters a culture of transparency. Channels are available for employees to report violations or misconduct anonymously or directly to HR or Compliance.

<!-- evidence:0aaaeb2262ea73f3c398468e source:word/document.xml#p00063 -->
9. Employee Termination and Offboarding

<!-- evidence:ed79e432ca060b4448686125 source:word/document.xml#p00064 -->
Employee termination may occur through voluntary resignation, involuntary termination, mutual separation, the end of a fixed-term contract, or retirement. Regardless of the reason for separation, the company follows a consistent and documented offboarding process to safeguard organizational assets, data, and continuity of operations.

<!-- evidence:8e132d8d691b879c75a9d05f source:word/document.xml#p00065 -->
Employees intending to resign must provide written notice in accordance with the notice period specified in their employment agreement. In cases of involuntary termination, the People Operations Officer, in coordination with the employee’s manager and, where applicable, Legal and Compliance, determines the effective last working day and ensures the process is conducted fairly and in compliance with applicable employment law.

<!-- evidence:424b56d1896e986af2f5b863 source:word/document.xml#p00066 -->
Offboarding is coordinated by the People Operations Officer using a documented checklist that covers revocation of access to systems, applications, and physical premises; deactivation of email and other organizational accounts; retrieval of company-issued assets such as laptops, mobile devices, identification badges, and keys; and removal from distribution lists and collaboration tools. For involuntary terminations or departures involving elevated risk, access is revoked on or before the last working day in coordination with IT Security.

<!-- evidence:6f3c431d5520949f460cd239 source:word/document.xml#p00067 -->
Departing employees are required to complete a knowledge transfer, handing over pending work, documentation, and system credentials to their manager or a designated successor to ensure continuity of business operations. Where feasible, an exit interview is conducted to gather feedback and to reiterate the individual’s ongoing confidentiality, data protection, and non-disclosure obligations, which survive termination of employment as set out in the applicable NDA and employment agreement.

<!-- evidence:fa0a5293f5bfe8d6b884bdfa source:word/document.xml#p00068 -->
Final settlement of dues, including accrued but unused leave, pending reimbursements, and any other compensation owed, is processed by HR and Finance in accordance with applicable law and company policy, within the timeframe required by local regulations. All offboarding activities, including access revocation, asset return, and exit acknowledgments, are documented and retained in the employee’s personnel file for audit and compliance purposes. The full offboarding checklist, covering HR and compliance updates, IT access revocation, asset recovery, and data handling, is provided in Annexure B: Employee Offboarding Checklist.

9. Contractual and Third-Party Responsibilities

<!-- evidence:579112afe0e7687d0b5f963c source:word/document.xml#p00071 -->
All third-party vendors, contractors, and service providers engaged by the company must adhere to the same disciplinary expectations as internal employees. Contracts must explicitly define the information security responsibilities of all involved parties and include clauses that mandate compliance with organizational security policies.

<!-- evidence:56161c3bdad83cc04652982d source:word/document.xml#p00072 -->
Third-party access is only granted after completion of the onboarding process, signature of contractual agreements, and review of access requirements. Periodic audits and performance reviews assess third-party compliance.
