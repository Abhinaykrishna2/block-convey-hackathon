<!-- evidence:b18af752775c12d8332a9e9e source:Guidance!A2 -->

<!-- evidence:e44fb2cd9a33f4165f15ec1a source:Guidance!A3 -->

<!-- evidence:7b0b5f773f575216cc223614 source:Guidance!A4 -->

<!-- evidence:dbefefc151c4a67f8153cb7a source:Guidance!A5 -->
4. Perform a review of the access levels and confirm if the access is appropriate of needs to change

<!-- evidence:4ff3e0a4812453244428fff5 source:Guidance!A6 -->
5. Log the date on which the access review was completed and person who completed it
